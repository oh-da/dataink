# Narrative Flow and Repetition — Application Guide

## Flow Options

Choose based on audience context:

- **Chronological:** Walk through the analysis step by step. Use when building credibility with a new audience or when the process matters.
- **Lead with the Ending:** Open with the recommendation, then provide evidence. Use when the audience is time-constrained or already trusts your work.

## Spoken vs. Written

- **Live presentations:** Slides stay sparse. Your voice carries the connective tissue.
- **Written reports / circulated decks:** State the takeaway explicitly on every page — no reader should have to guess the point.

## Repetition Strategy

**"Bing, Bang, Bongo"** — reinforce at three points:
1. **Bing (Preview):** Executive summary previewing what you'll cover and what you'll ask for.
2. **Bang (Detail):** Full data, conflict, and evidence.
3. **Bongo (Recap):** Restate key findings + repeat the call to action.

**Sound bites:** Craft short, memorable phrases capturing the core message. If the audience can repeat your main point in their own words afterward, the message stuck.
