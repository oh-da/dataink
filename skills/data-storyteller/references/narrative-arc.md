# Three-Act Narrative Arc — Application Guide

Structure data communication as: setup → conflict → resolution. This guide provides the specific questions to answer in each act.

## Act I: The Beginning (Setup)

Answer these before drafting:

1. **Who must act, and what world are they in?** Frame the audience as the protagonist. Establish the relevant context (time frame, market, organizational situation).
2. **What is at stake?** Name the gap between "what is" and "what could be." Without a clear stake, the audience has no reason to stay engaged.

## Act II: The Middle (Conflict)

Build the case through two contrasting forces:

- **Cost of standing still:** Use data to make the problem tangible — what deteriorates, what is lost, what risk grows without action.
- **Payoff of acting:** Present the recommended solution, quantify benefits, and connect directly to the gap from Act I. Explain why *this audience* is the right group to act.

## Act III: The End (Resolution)

- **Circle back:** Remind of the problem and urgency from Act I.
- **State the call to action:** Be explicit. "Approve the $200k reallocation by Friday" — not "let's discuss."
