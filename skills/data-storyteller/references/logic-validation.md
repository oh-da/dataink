# Logic Validation Tactics

Use these checks as a final review before delivery.

## 1. Horizontal Logic
Extract every slide title (or section header) and read them in sequence as a paragraph.
- **Pass:** The paragraph tells a coherent, complete story.
- **Fail:** Gaps or non-sequiturs → restructure the narrative.
- **Prerequisite:** This only works with action titles (insight-driven), not topic labels.

## 2. Vertical Logic
Within any single slide or page:
- Title states the insight.
- Chart proves it.
- Annotation explains it.
- Nothing else is present. If an element doesn't support the title, move it to an appendix or remove it.

## 3. Reverse Storyboarding
After completing the deck, jot down the single takeaway from each page. Compare against your original outline.
- **Match:** Narrative is working.
- **Divergence:** Pages need to be added, removed, or reordered.

## 4. Fresh Perspective
Show the finished communication to someone unfamiliar with the topic. Ask them to summarize the main takeaways without your voiceover. Where they stumble is where the narrative needs tightening.

## If Validation Fails
Return to the narrative arc (Step 2 in the workflow) and restructure before attempting validation again. Do not proceed with a broken narrative.
